- Fixed: *Keep cover only* on a large selection, such as Ctrl+A over every
  stacked picture, said the preview could not be read and would not let you
  continue. It now works on a selection of any size, shows how far it has got
  while it runs, and is still undone in one go with Ctrl+Z. Undoing it over
  hundreds of stacks is also much faster.
- Fixed: when the *Keep cover only* preview cannot be read, the dialog now says
  why and offers *Try again* when asking again can help, instead of telling you
  to close it and start over. Screen readers now announce that message.
