- Fixed: switching views quickly could leave pictures from the view you had
  just left in the grid. Leaving a view now stops its loading, and the grid
  only shows the view on screen.
- Fixed: when part of a large view failed to load, the grid could show a
  shorter list as though it were complete. It now says the pictures couldn't
  be loaded and offers Try again.
- Fixed: with the Images or Videos filter on, cards could be drawn in the
  wrong place while a view was still loading. Scrolling with either filter on
  is also lighter on large libraries.
- Fixed: a change made while the grid was loading or had just loaded, such as
  emptying the scrapheap, could leave pictures that were already gone on
  screen. The grid now reads the view again after the change.
